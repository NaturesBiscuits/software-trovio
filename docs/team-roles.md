## Working Agreements

- Backlog items must include priority, story points, and acceptance criteria before sprint selection.
- Sprint 1 work should stay focused on discovery, filtering, onboarding, and listing detail flows.
- Each Sprint 1 story must have a named owner in `docs/sprint-1-plan.md`.
- Each story is complete only after its acceptance criteria pass QA review.
- Planning updates for Week 2 must include backlog, Sprint 1 plan, schedule, and roles.

## Communication Cadence

| Meeting | Purpose | Suggested Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sprint Planning | Select sprint stories and confirm capacity. | PM/Scrum |
| Daily Check-in | Share progress, blockers, and next steps. | PM/Scrum |
| Backlog Refinement | Add details, update estimates, and reprioritize stories. | PM/Scrum |
| Sprint Review | Demonstrate completed work against acceptance criteria. | PM/Scrum |
| Retrospective | Discuss process improvements for the next sprint. | QA Lead |
