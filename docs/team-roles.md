## Working Agreements

- Backlog items must include priority, story points, and acceptance criteria before sprint selection.
- Sprint 1 work should stay focused on discovery, filtering, onboarding, and listing detail flows.
- Each story is considered complete only after the assigned acceptance criteria pass review.
- Unrelated changes should not be included in planning commits.
- Product and technical decisions should be reflected in `docs/backlog.md` or `docs/sprint-1-plan.md` when they affect scope.

## Communication Cadence

| Meeting | Purpose | Suggested Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sprint Planning | Select sprint stories and confirm capacity. | Scrum Master |
| Daily Check-in | Share progress, blockers, and next steps. | Scrum Master |
| Backlog Refinement | Add details, update estimates, and reprioritize stories. | Product Owner |
| Sprint Review | Demonstrate completed work against acceptance criteria. | Product Owner |
| Retrospective | Discuss process improvements for the next sprint. | Scrum Master |
